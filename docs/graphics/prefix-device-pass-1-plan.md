## 1. Device (lens only)

One line. Two section kinds as D draws them: WORD, POINTER.  
A star is not a row. It is repeated passage through the same word-variable already on \(L\).  
The prefix \(L_{<n}\) *is* the dictionary for the next write. There is no outer lexicon.  
Recursion: a POINTER may name a WORD or a POINTER already on the line. That is D3 / D6. That is also “recursive to the entire lace”: the next cell is always \(\Phi\) of the whole current substrate.

**This pass may use that lens to classify panels. It may not promote the lens to Core.**
