# Adjacent reference

These books are **not** the source of record for laceArc. The source of record is `docs/graphics/`.

They sit next to the model: same series nexus (knots as a meeting place for structure), not imported axioms. Do not copy Reidemeister moves, bracket polynomials, spin networks, or Rowlands’ foundations into Core because a passage feels similar to wire, star, or strand.

Do not commit the PDF binaries to this public repository. World Scientific holds copyright.

## Holdings (local / project workspace only)

Keep purchased or otherwise lawfully held copies off `main`. Suggested local names if you keep files beside a private checkout:

- `refs/local/kauffman-knots-and-physics-4ed.pdf`
- `refs/local/rowlands-zero-to-infinity.pdf`

Those paths are gitignored.

## Catalog

### 1. Louis H. Kauffman — *Knots and Physics*, 4th edition

- Series on Knots and Everything, Vol. 53
- World Scientific, 2013
- ISBN 978-981-4383-00-4 (hardcover), 978-981-4383-01-1 (paperback)
- Author affiliation as printed: University of Illinois, Chicago

Why it is adjacent: Part I is combinatorial (bracket, state model, Yang–Baxter, Temperley–Lieb, 3-manifold invariants). Part II goes under Reidemeister moves, hitches, coloring, Potts, spin networks, strings, DNA. Lace talks in wire, over/under, threading, and projection. That is metaphor kinship, not a license to implement a knot polynomial.

### 2. Peter Rowlands — *Zero to Infinity: The Foundations of Physics*

- Series on Knots and Everything, Vol. 41
- World Scientific, 2007
- ISBN 978-981-270-914-1 / 981-270-914-2
- Author affiliation as printed: University of Liverpool, UK

Why it is adjacent: same series, editor-in-charge Louis H. Kauffman. The series preface states knots as a pivot among physics, mathematics, logic, linguistics, philosophy, biology. Lace is a language-structure model in that neighborhood. It is not Rowlands’ physics stack.

## Series frame (from Vol. 41 front matter)

The series is polarized around knot theory as a meeting place for apparently separate ideas. That sentence is the only series claim laceArc needs. It does not make Kauffman or Rowlands into Lace Core.

## Rule for readers and bots

If a sentence in either book would change WORD, POINTER, star, or the governing rule, discard the sentence for Core and keep it as reading notes. Pointer emission remains a `[GAP]` until written in `docs/pointer-emission.md` from the four graphics, not from these volumes.
