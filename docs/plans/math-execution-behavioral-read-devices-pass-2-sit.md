## 0. The correction produced by sitting in the originals

The three references are not best understood as three ordinary state machines waiting to be translated into \(\mathcal D_\theta=(Q,G,\Omega,\Delta,\omega)\).

They are three different **descriptive architectures**:

1. **HCC-A** describes how a holder compiles constraint-bearing input into mutable local story/meaning/emotion/identity and observable behavior.
2. **The Coffee Cup** describes causal position under time: enabling conditions, initiation, live trajectory/control window, realized outcome, and causal aftermath. Its strategic point is explicitly to hold all five causal positions at once.
3. **Where the Water Is Loud / Dual Proofing Systems** describes two native proofing systems with different objects and failure detectors, plus an interface that translates between them.

All three can be hosted by the generic S9 chassis.

But formalizing them faithfully requires an intermediate question:

> **What kind of thing is each source statement measuring, and from which information domain could that measurement honestly be obtained?**

That is an observability / identifiability problem, not yet a source-specific implementation problem.

## 2. HCC-A — what kind of descriptive object is it?

### 2.1 It is a recurrent local compiler architecture

The source's causal chain is not just a list of labels.

It asserts:

\[
R \to I \to (RIC\parallel PFC) \to C \to L_{story}\to M\to E\to ISL\to B\to R'.
\]

Important distinctions:

- **Reality \(R\)** is objective and constraining but not directly accessible.
- **Interface \(I\)** converts Reality into internal signals.
- **RIC and PFC receive the same signals in parallel.**
- RIC aims to preserve external structure.
- PFC applies prior/template interpretation.
- both feed compilation.
- Story/Meaning/Emotion/Identity are holder-local mutable states.
- Behavior is the only directly observable part of the loop.
- Behavior alters later Reality/input.

### 2.2 The source has more than one descriptive layer

HCC-A contains at least four different descriptive products:

1. **component architecture** — R, I, RIC, PFC, C, L, M, E, ISL, B;
2. **runtime causal/state-machine flow** — S0–S7;
3. **parameter space** — templates, priority stack, pruning, arbitration, meaning style, emotion style, identity mode;
4. **Six-Act diagnostic stack** — questions used to inspect an event/interaction;
5. **YAML schema** — an example/configuration/trace representation.

These must not be collapsed.

In particular:

\[
\text{Six-Act diagnostic description}
\neq
\text{S0--S7 runtime state machine}
\]

and:

\[
\text{YAML serialization}
\neq
\text{required internal storage}.
\]

### 2.3 HCC pressure on S9

A later HCC device needs more than "a state \(Q_H\)."

Its factorization must preserve:

\[
\text{same admitted structural packet}
\to
\begin{cases}
RIC\text{-like evidence path}\\
PFC\text{-like prior-conditioned path}
\end{cases}
\]

before compilation.

This means Pass 2 must map the distinction between:

- **admitted evidence**;
- **interpretive transformation**;
- **local latent state**;
- **diagnostic readout**;
- **observable behavior**.

A generic \(\Omega(L,q,g)\) is expressive enough, but a source-specific HCC lift must prove it has not hidden prior-conditioned selection inside the allegedly preserved evidence path.

### 2.4 HCC identifiability boundary

From the source itself:

- Behavior is directly observable.
- Emotion is not transmissible.
- only structure crosses minds.
- Reality is not directly accessible by a mind.
- local Story/Meaning/Identity are internal.

Therefore another device cannot infer the true \(M,E,ISL,L_{story}\) of a holder merely from shared Lace structure without extra premises.

This is not an implementation inconvenience.

It is part of the source's model.

## 3. Coffee Cup — the single-state reading is too small

### 3.1 The five stages are heterogeneous causal roles

The source names:

\[
Conditions \to Release \to Trajectory \to Impact \to Aftermath.
\]

But these are not five homogeneous states.

- **Conditions** are an enabling field.
- **Release** is an initiating boundary/event.
- **Trajectory** is an interval with a shrinking intervention set.
- **Impact** is a resolved outcome / reality check.
- **Aftermath** is a causal transformation of later conditions.

Treating them as only:

\[
\chi\in\{C,R,T,I,A\}
\]

would preserve chronology but lose their different causal jobs.

### 3.2 Strategic literacy explicitly requires simultaneous representation

The source's strategic theory says planning is the ability to **hold all five stages at once while time still exists**.

That is not a one-hot FSM requirement.

It points toward a simultaneous causal description such as a future candidate:

\[
\mathcal C_e
=
(C_e,R_e,T_e,I_e,A_e)
\]

where the five coordinates have different types and some may be incomplete/current/future-facing.

This is a **candidate device lift**, not a source equation.

Pass 2 should determine the correct mathematical shape before adopting it.

### 3.3 Trajectory is not historical sequence alone

Trajectory asks:

> When could this outcome still be altered?

That requires more than "what happened in what order."

It uses:

- elapsed or event-relevant time;
- signals visible at the time;
- possible interventions;
- socially costly interventions;
- changing mitigation capacity;
- uncertainty about whether intervention is necessary.

Thus part of the Cup device is **counterfactual/control-state**:

\[
\text{what could still be changed}
\]

rather than merely historical observation.

Lace may provide ordered evidence.

It does not automatically provide the admissible intervention set.

### 3.4 Impact is a reality constraint, not just the last observed row

The source uses Impact to force a reality check:

\[
\text{what actually happened}
\neq
\text{what might have happened}.
\]

A future Cup device must distinguish:

- recorded Lace evidence of impact;
- external evidence of impact;
- local interpretation of impact.

It cannot promote "latest Lace row" into "Reality resolved" by definition.

### 3.5 Aftermath recursion is not historical rewrite

Aftermath changes future thresholds, narratives and conditions.

The source language says it "rewrites conditions," not that it rewrites the historical event.

S9/RD-1 already gives the correct project fence:

\[
\text{Aftermath local/context change}
\neq
\text{mutation of prior Lace}.
\]

## 4. Water — not one device, but two native systems plus an interface

### 4.1 System A and System B have different native objects

**Proof Ledger:**

- OBS;
- DELTA;
- UNK;
- INVALID.

Unit of progress:

\[
OBS\ created
\to
DELTA\ created
\to
UNK\ resolved.
\]

Failure detector: INVALID / non-computable input.

**River Proofing:**

- Banks;
- Rapids;
- Delta / Crossing.

Unit of progress:

\[
Rapids \to Delta \to Crossing.
\]

Failure detector: too loud / no crossing conditions.

These are not two labels for one state space.

The source explicitly says the interface lets each party/system "speak in their native system."

### 4.2 Structural isomorphism is mapping, not identity

The source states compatibility around one underlying requirement:

> crossing requires shared reference objects and bounded unknowns.

Then gives approximate mappings:

- Banks \(\approx\) separate OBS inventories;
- Rapids \(\approx\) high-rate talk with missing anchors + unresolved UNK;
- Delta \(\approx\) paired pointable observations + declared unknowns.

This should be modeled as an interface/translation relation.

It should not be collapsed into:

\[
Banks = OBS_A
\]

or:

\[
River\ Delta = Lace\ relation.
\]

### 4.3 Water is inherently multi-holder

The source explicitly permits:

- my OBS;
- your OBS;
- separate inventories;
- DELTA only when observations are paired.

So a faithful Water device needs holder-indexed state:

\[
\{Q_h\}_{h\in H}
\]

or an equivalent construction, plus shared interface state.

A single undifferentiated \(Q_W\) is mathematically capable of storing this, but hides the source's separation unless the factorization is explicit.

### 4.4 The interface itself is a mathematical object

The source's Delta Bridge has:

- shared states S0–S4;
- F1 Locate;
- F2 Anchor;
- F3 Pair;
- F4 Unknowns;
- F5 Reject.

The interface is not merely another state variable.

Its stated job is translation/routing between the two systems.

That suggests a later source-specific formalization should preserve three objects:

\[
ProofLedger,\quad River,\quad Bridge
\]

rather than immediately replacing them with one monolithic transition function.

### 4.5 Water contains explicit source-open design degrees

The source itself says it still does not have:

1. OBS pointer format;
2. INVALID stop-word/pattern list;
3. UNK operational rule / budget.

Those are not bugs in the repo.

They are source-declared opens and must remain visible in any mathematical device.

This is a Pass-2 planning inference, not a claim that the sources themselves say "orthogonal axes."

A useful current reading is:

### HCC-A

**Axis:** holder-local compilation and behavior.

Question:

> How does constraint-bearing input become local story/meaning/emotion/identity and behavior?

### Coffee Cup

**Axis:** causal-temporal position and intervention.

Question:

> Where is an event in its causal shape, what is still alterable, what actually happened, and how does it change the next start?

### Water

**Axis:** intersubjective computability and crossing.

Question:

> Can multiple holders point at enough shared structure to compute a disagreement or cross yet?

These axes can inspect the same Lace prefix while producing non-identical descriptions.

That is exactly what the S9 generic chassis makes possible.

## 6. A new observability / identifiability court is needed

Before source-specific devices are built, every source variable should be classified by where its value may legitimately come from.

Pass 2 should test a classification like:

### SEE-L — Lace-derived

The value can be derived from the named Lace prefix using current lawful reads.

Examples:
- recorded order;
- retained WORD/POINTER structure;
- Reach/path;
- root/star/sampling/search evidence.

### SEE-G — external/context

The value requires supplied non-Lace context.

Examples:
- physical/social clock;
- current environment;
- intervention affordances;
- Interface signal not already recorded;
- live conversational pressure.

### SEE-Q — device/holder-local

The value is a local state or interpretation.

Examples:
- HCC Story/Meaning/Emotion/Identity;
- Cup attention/aftermath model;
- Water local proof inventory state.

### SEE-H — other-holder / multi-holder relational

The value requires another holder's distinct state/input or an explicit pairing.

Examples:
- Water OBS_A vs OBS_B pairing;
- acknowledgement that an anchor is shared;
- HCC claim about another person's internal state, if attempted.

### SEE-CF — counterfactual/model-dependent

The value is about possible actions/outcomes rather than only observed history.

Examples:
- Cup's intervention window;
- "what could still be altered";
- "what intervention was possible but costly."

### SEE-U — unresolved/source-open

The source itself leaves the relevant rule unspecified or required evidence is missing.

Examples:
- Water OBS pointer format;
- INVALID matcher;
- UNK budget;
- any claim whose required witness is absent.

The categories may overlap.

The key rule to test is:

\[
SEE-G,SEE-Q,SEE-H,SEE-CF,SEE-U
\not\subseteq
SEE-L
\]

without explicit evidence.

## 7. S9 survives, but Pass 2 must test its internal factorization

The sit does **not** find a need to throw away:

\[
\mathcal D_\theta=(Q,G,\Omega,\Delta,\omega).
\]

It does find four things Pass 2 must test before full devices are written.

### 7.1 Evidence vs interpretation

HCC requires the structural input to remain separately available after PFC-shaped interpretation.

Pass 2 must determine whether a source-specific read packet needs an invariant like:

\[
o=(s,\nu)
\]

with:

\[
s_{evidence}
\]

preserved independently of local interpretation.

### 7.2 Description vs transition

Some source machinery is diagnostic/descriptive rather than a causal state change:

- HCC Six-Act questions;
- Cup strategic literacy;
- Water River location diagnosis.

Pass 2 must determine whether \(\Delta\)'s typed output is sufficient to represent a pure descriptive readout cleanly, or whether a later source-specific formalism should distinguish:

\[
measurement/readout
\neq
local transition.
\]

Do not re-expand the generic tuple merely for aesthetic symmetry.

### 7.3 Simultaneous factorization

Coffee Cup's "hold all five" and Water's dual systems show that a source device may require a **product/factorized description** instead of one active discrete state.

Pass 2 should derive the needed composition forms before choosing one.

### 7.4 Holder indexing

Water makes multiple local inventories source-required.

HCC's non-transfer rule also makes holder identity important.

Pass 2 should make multi-holder indexing explicit where needed instead of burying it inside an opaque \(Q\).
