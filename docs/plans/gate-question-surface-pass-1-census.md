## 3. What closed each closed row

`pointer-emission.md`'s own editing rules distinguish **`CLOSED`** (*"picture or D1 already settles it"*) from **`SPOKEN`** (*"the human wrote the sentence in-repo"*). A row marked `CLOSED` that does not name its authority cannot be checked against the rule that produced it.

| Rows marked `CLOSED` in S1 | **9** |

**At least two of the six silent rows rest partly on human rulings, not on the picture.** The file's own basis for the constructor reads: *"D2 presents the two-reference POINTER schema; D1 is 6/6 binary; D6 permits POINTER→POINTER; **H5** says arbitrary existing portions can become one point; **H6** gives the n+1 grounding construction."* `H5` and `H6` are `SPOKEN`. The row says `CLOSED`.

**This is not an error. It is an undrawn distinction**, and it lands on the one act the file exists for: **acceptance would accept picture-closures and ruling-closures under a single word.**
