- `[SOURCE]` — directly stated/drawn by Graphics A–D;
- `[HUMAN]` — explicit later human ruling;
- `[DERIVED]` — mathematical/structural consequence of SOURCE/HUMAN;
- `[LAB]` — experimentally demonstrated machinery, not source law;
- `[CANDIDATE]` — machinery admitted to the lab but not architecture;
- `[VIEW]` — read/projection only;
- `[FORBID]` — anti-model / prohibited interpretation;
- `[GAP]` — required behavior not established.
