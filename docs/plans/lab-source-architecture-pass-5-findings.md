## 0. Result first

The research family is **not silent**.

It contains:
- real physical stability thresholds;
- explicit algebraic validity predicates;
- explicit selectors and argmax rules;
- interpolation/non-uniqueness theorems;
- evidence-backed candidate retention;
- decisive candidate selection.

But **zero research mechanisms pass all five admission gates** without one of:
- changing the source mechanism's original purpose;
- inventing a source-domain → Lace adapter;
- requiring unavailable semantic/learned state;
- returning the wrong output type;
- using full/future trajectory information.

Therefore **zero research candidates are added to the retention harness in Pass 5**.

That distinction matters:

```
research has RM-shaped machinery
        !=
research supplies an admissible Lace RM candidate
```

## 13. What Pass 5 actually learned

The research family splits the RM problem into two layers:

```
GENERIC SELECTION / RETENTION MACHINERY
    research has plenty

LEGAL LACE-SOCKET INPUT / OUTPUT CORRESPONDENCE
    research does not supply it
```

AgentScope is the clearest proof:
- evidence-backed retention of multiple candidates exists;
- later decisive selection exists;
- the source even separates detection from final attribution.

But its evidence is built from an annotated semantic graph and neural judges over trajectories. Lace does not currently expose that object at RM-A, and inventing it would be the missing mechanism in another costume.

Kauffman supplies a real physical threshold. Xiao supplies real selectors. Rowlands supplies real validity algebra. Petersen supplies rigorous interpolation/reconstruction theory.

None supplies the missing **mapping from sampling-derived Lace evidence to a lawful retain/materialize/order act**.

RESEARCH SUPPLIES RM-SHAPED MACHINERY
